META NEWS AUTOMATION APP

Data Deletion Instructions

Effective November 23, 2026

The automation does not create reader accounts or import Facebook user profiles, follower lists, comments, or Messenger conversations. It does keep operational records about public news stories and Page posts.

SEND A PRIVATE REQUEST

Contact Himal Samachar on Facebook

Send a private message and ask for a “Data deletion request.” Include the article URL or Facebook post permalink involved and a short description of the information you want reviewed.

Message Himal Samachar ↗

Do not include your password, access token, or unrelated sensitive documents. You need to sign in to Facebook or Messenger to send a message. This website has no separate email address or deletion form. If Messenger is unavailable to you, use the Page’s private contact options.

What happens next

  1. A Page administrator reviews the request and checks whether the automation has a matching story or correction record.
  2. If a matching app record is identified and removal is appropriate, an authorized administrator can remove it from the private Supabase database.
  3. For a public Facebook post, include its permalink. A separate Page action may be needed.

There is no self-service deletion tool or automated deletion callback in this app, and no fixed response or completion time is stated. Removing an app record does not delete copies held by Meta, Google, Supabase, GitHub, Radio Nepal, or another service. Requests about those services’ own records must also go to the relevant service.

Operational records can include source URLs, generated summaries, Meta post IDs, statuses, and timestamps. The app does not maintain an individual Facebook user-profile database.
