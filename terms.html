META NEWS AUTOMATION APP

Terms of Service

Effective November 23, 2026

These terms describe the Himal Samachar news automation used to prepare and publish attributed news summaries to the Himal Samachar Facebook Page. The app does not offer public login or reader accounts.

Service scope

The production schedule currently collects from Radio Nepal’s official public RSS feed, ranks eligible stories, and uses Google Gemini to draft original Nepali factual summaries. A post may include Radio Nepal attribution and a link to the original article. Other configured publishers are not enabled in the current production schedule.

Editorial content

Summaries are intended to restate supported facts in original wording. Allegations and uncertainty should remain attributed. The automation applies evidence, numeric-accuracy, duplicate, and wording-overlap checks, but a summary can still be incomplete or incorrect. Readers should consult the linked source for the full report and context.

The automation publishes only when it finds a fresh, eligible story. A scheduled run may publish nothing. No posting frequency or uninterrupted availability is promised.

Sources and third-party services

Source articles and linked websites remain subject to their publishers’ terms. The app does not grant permission to copy article text, headlines, photographs, audio, or video. Meta, Google, Supabase, GitHub, and source publishers provide separate services under their own terms and privacy policies.

Corrections and requests

To report a possible factual error or request review of a Page post, send a private message to the Himal Samachar Facebook Page with the post URL and supporting source. Requests are reviewed manually; this page does not promise a particular outcome or response time.

Updates

These terms may be updated if the automation or its operation changes. The effective date above identifies the current version.
